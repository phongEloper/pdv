Commissions Policy

These terms outline the default rules for Roblox scripting, systems, tooling, UI logic, and game feature commissions. Project-specific terms may be adjusted in writing before work begins.

1. Scope

The feature list, expected behavior, integrations, and delivery format must be confirmed before production starts.

2. Payment

Payments are priced in USD. A deposit may be required before work begins, especially for large systems or urgent delivery.

3. Revisions

Small revisions related to the agreed scope are included. New mechanics, major redesigns, or extra integrations are quoted separately.

4. Delivery

Delivery may include Roblox place files, Rojo project files, ModuleScripts, documentation, or setup notes depending on the commission.

5. Refunds

Refunds are not guaranteed after production has started. Partial refunds may be considered only when the unfinished work can be fairly separated.

6. Ownership

The client may use the delivered work in the agreed project. Pdv may show non-sensitive work samples in a portfolio unless privacy is agreed first.

7. Communication

Clear references, test cases, and timely feedback help keep delivery stable. Delayed feedback may move the delivery date.
